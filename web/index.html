Which homepages and dates are covered?

Internet Archive’s CDX index lists preserved responses. Several captures can have identical content. Each year shows successful HTML captures and the number of distinct content digests. A blank year in a retrieved index means no qualifying response was returned at the selected addresses. A network with no index is labelled unavailable, not zero. Commercial collections have explicit end years so a reused domain does not silently become evidence about its earlier service. A network could have operated under other paths or hostnames.

For selected captures the tool retrieves the archived HTML, displays its page title, and checks for a short list of service names in its visible text. Missing terms and unavailable text leave the services unverified. Historical milestones link to contemporary or first-party documentation. Nearest capture links select the closest saved year-edge response to January 1 of the milestone year; they do not claim the page was captured on the event date. Old domains are opened through the archive, never as live retired sites.
