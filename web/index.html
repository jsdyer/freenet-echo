Which homepages and dates are covered?

Internet Archive’s CDX index lists preserved responses. Several captures can have identical content. Each year shows successful HTML captures and the number of distinct content digests. A blank year means this index returned no qualifying response at our selected addresses. A network could have operated under other paths or hostnames.

For selected captures the tool retrieves the archived HTML, displays its page title, and checks for a short list of service names in its visible text. Missing terms and unavailable text leave the services unverified. The historical milestones link independently to the networks’ own documentation.
